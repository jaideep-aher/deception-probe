How DeceptionProbe Works: Inside the Residual Stream

01

Linear Representation Hypothesis

Concepts like truthfulness and deception are encoded as linear directions in the transformer residual stream. By projecting activation vectors \(h_l\) onto a calibrated unit vector \(v_{\text{truth}}\), we measure internal agreement before text is sampled.

02

Contrast-Consistent Search

Using paired statements \((x, \neg x)\), we discover truthfulness subspaces without human labels by enforcing logical consistency: \(P(x) + P(\neg x) = 1\) while maximizing the informativeness of the predictions.

03

Causal Activation Steering

Detection alone is passive. DeceptionProbe closes the loop by adding \( \alpha \cdot v_{\text{truth}} \) into the forward pass during generation, allowing humans to actively rescue models from sycophancy in real time.
